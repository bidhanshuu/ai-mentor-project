Vocabulary retention at the N4 level improves more through active recall than passive rereading. Students should test themselves on meaning, pronunciation, and sentence usage instead of only reviewing lists.

Spaced repetition and repeated retrieval improve speed during viva and written exams. Short, frequent revision sessions are usually more effective than one long memorization session.
