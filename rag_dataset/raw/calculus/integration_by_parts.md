Integration by parts is applied when the integrand contains a product of two terms and one of them becomes simpler after differentiation. The standard formula is integral of u dv equals u multiplied by v minus integral of v du.

Students often use the LIATE idea to choose u. If u is chosen badly, the second integral becomes harder and the method fails. In exam preparation, students should practice deciding u and dv before starting the calculation.
